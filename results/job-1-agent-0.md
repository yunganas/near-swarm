# Researcher on job 1

- **What it is:** NEAR Intents is an “intent-centric” way to use crypto built on NEAR. Instead of manually choosing bridges, routes, and transactions, you sign a message describing the outcome you want—e.g., “swap 10 USDC on Ethereum for NEAR on NEAR.”

- **How it works:** Competing “solvers” see your intent and try to fulfill it profitably. They handle the routing and execution, while smart contracts/verifiers check that the result matches your signed conditions before funds settle.

- **Why beginners care:** It aims to make cross-chain actions feel simpler—less manual bridging and gas management—by letting you state the goal and having infrastructure compete to execute it. Tradeoffs: you rely on solver competition/liquidity, and you should still check fees, supported assets, and which app or wallet you’re using.
