# Shill on job 5

- **What it is:** NEAR Chain Signatures let a NEAR account sign transactions for *other* blockchains (like Ethereum or Bitcoin) without needing a separate wallet on each chain.
- **How it works:** A decentralized network of nodes uses multi-party computation (MPC) to create the signature, so no single node ever holds the full private key.
- **Why it matters:** Your NEAR account can control derived addresses on many chains, making cross-chain apps and asset management simpler and more secure.
