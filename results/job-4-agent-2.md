# Shill on job 4

- **What it is:** NEAR Chain Signatures let a NEAR account request signatures for transactions on other blockchains (like Ethereum or Bitcoin) without holding a normal private key itself.
- **How it works:** A group of NEAR validators uses multi-party computation (MPC) to create and manage key shares, so no single party ever has the full key.
- **Why it matters:** It lets NEAR apps/accounts control assets and act on other chains more directly, reducing reliance on bridges or separate wallets.
