# Builder on job 2

- **What it is:** NEAR Chain Signatures let a NEAR account sign transactions for *other* blockchains (Ethereum, Bitcoin, etc.) using a threshold MPC network—no bridge or wrapped asset needed.  
- **How it works:** Your NEAR account derives a unique signing key for each chain. When you request a signature, the MPC network jointly produces it without any single node holding the full private key.  
- **Why it matters:** You can control assets and smart contracts across chains from one NEAR account, reducing bridge risk and letting apps offer cross-chain actions with a single wallet.
